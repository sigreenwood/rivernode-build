RiverNode v3a  -  fabrication notes
===================================

Board            182.0 x 136.0 mm, rectangular, 4x M3 mounting holes
Layers           2
Thickness        1.6 mm
Copper weight    1 oz  (35 um)  -- NOT optional, see below
Surface finish   HASL is fine (all through-hole except one SOT-23-6)
Mask / legend    any colour
Min track        0.30 mm
Min clearance    0.25 mm
Min drill        0.30 mm  (exactly one hole; everything else is 0.40 mm or larger)
Min annular ring 0.15 mm

WHY 1 oz MATTERS
The 12 V path from J1 through Q1, the current shunt, J12 and Q4 to the modem is
routed at 1.5 mm because the modem pulls up to 2.2 A in transmit bursts. That
width assumes 1 oz outer copper. On 0.5 oz the same trace is undersized and will
run hot. If the fab's default is 0.5 oz, pay for 1 oz.

BEFORE PAYING
Upload these and read the fab's own DFM report. It checks things no desktop tool
does: annular ring against their real process, copper slivers, acid traps, mask
slivers, legend over pads. It is free and it is the only genuinely independent
check on this design.
