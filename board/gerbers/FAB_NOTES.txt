RiverNode v3.5  -  fabrication notes  (Qwiic build, 27 Nov 2026)
=====================================

Board            182.0 x 136.0 mm, rectangular, 4x M3 mounting holes (same outline as v3a)
Layers           2
Thickness        1.6 mm
Copper weight    1 oz  (35 um)  -- NOT optional, see below
Surface finish   HASL is fine (all through-hole except U3, SOT-23-6)
Mask / legend    any colour. Legend on BOTH sides: the back now carries pin-1 marks,
                 count marks, power-pin names and test-point names
Min track        0.30 mm
Min clearance    0.25 mm
Min drill        0.40 mm
Min annular ring 0.15 mm

WHY 1 oz MATTERS
The 12 V path from J1 through Q1, J12 and Q4 to the
modem is routed at 1.5 mm because the modem pulls up to 2.2 A in transmit bursts. That
width assumes 1 oz outer copper. On 0.5 oz the same trace is undersized and will run
hot. If the fab's default is 0.5 oz, pay for 1 oz.

532 holes: 221 component pads (including 16 test points and the E1 earth stud),
311 vias (9 signal, 302 ground stitching).

BEFORE PAYING
1. Check J3's 35.56 mm row spacing on FIT_CHECK_1to1.pdf (still unmeasured).
2. Upload these and read the fab's own DFM report. It checks things no desktop tool
   does: annular ring against their real process, copper slivers, acid traps, mask
   slivers, legend over pads. It is free and it is the only genuinely independent
   check on this design.
