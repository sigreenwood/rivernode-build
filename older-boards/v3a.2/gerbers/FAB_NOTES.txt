RiverNode v3a.2  -  fabrication notes
=====================================

Board            182.0 x 136.0 mm, rectangular, 4x M3 mounting holes
Layers           2
Thickness        1.6 mm
Copper weight    1 oz  (35 um)  -- NOT optional, see below
Surface finish   HASL is fine
Mask / legend    any colour, legend on BOTH sides
Min track        0.30 mm
Min clearance    0.25 mm (0.40 mm on the 12 V and probe nets)
Min drill        0.30 mm
Min annular ring 0.15 mm

v3a.2 is v3a, the board that has been made and run, with its tracks kept as
they were. Changes: the INA226 socket MOD3 is gone and its 12 V pins are joined
in copper; a service-reed terminal J20 is added; silkscreen corrected.

WHY 1 oz MATTERS
The 12 V path from J1 through Q1, J12 and Q4 to the modem is routed at 1.5 mm
because the modem pulls up to 2.2 A in transmit bursts. That width assumes 1 oz
outer copper. On 0.5 oz the same trace is undersized and will run hot.

SURFACE-MOUNT PARTS
U3 (SOT-23-6) only. Everything else is through-hole.
